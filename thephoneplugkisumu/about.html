Our Story

Built on the ground floor, for people who'd rather see it first

The Phone Plug Kisumu opened on the ground floor of Kisumu Mega City with one idea: buying a phone or laptop shouldn't mean scrolling through listings and hoping the photos match reality. We wanted a place where you could walk in, hold the actual device, ask a real question and get a straight answer.

Today our shop floor is stocked with smartphones from Samsung, Apple, Tecno, Infinix, Oppo, Vivo, Honor and realme, laptops from HP, Dell and Lenovo, and a full wall of accessories — chargers, power banks, cables, batteries, adapters, glass protectors and covers. Every device on our shelves is genuine, and every sale comes with a conversation, not just a receipt.

We're proud to be part of the growing line-up of shops inside Kisumu Mega City, and prouder still that so many of our customers are repeat visitors who bring their friends and family back with them.
